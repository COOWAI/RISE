# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of background,
identity, experience, or level of participation.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, precise, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect differing viewpoints and acknowledge mistakes.
- Keep reviews focused on the technical and scientific work.
- Protect private information and disclose conflicts of interest.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted attention, deliberate intimidation, and publication of
another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a public concern, open an issue and ask a maintainer to review it. For a confidential conduct report, use the
repository's GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting form and prefix the report title with `Conduct report:`. Do not
include sensitive details in a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, limit disclosure to people needed for resolution, and may remove
content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently exclude a participant. Retaliation
against a reporter is prohibited.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, code review, issue tracking, and public spaces where a participant is
representing the project.
